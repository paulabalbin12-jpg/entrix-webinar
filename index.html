Why it matters

What does revenue per MW really tell us about battery performance?

It's the number everyone quotes, and it makes batteries easy to rank. But the ranking doesn't explain why one battery earns more than another.

BESS developers

Comparing projects

Two projects can have similar expected revenue per MW on paper. Before you choose between them, you need to know what each number is based on.

Asset owners

Judging an operating asset

A high revenue per MW looks like strong performance, and it may be. The figure alone can't confirm it.
